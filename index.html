Our table

From Kabul,
with love.

Afghan food is a language of generosity: fragrant rice piled high, bread torn and shared, and one more plate arriving just when you thought the feast was complete.

At Saffron & Stone, we cook the dishes we grew up with—slowly, honestly, and with a little modern spirit. Every dumpling is folded by hand. Every spice blend starts in our kitchen. Every guest is welcomed like an old friend.
